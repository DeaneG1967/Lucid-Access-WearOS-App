Lucid Access is an unofficial WearOS Android watch companion app for Lucid's vehicles, allowing locking and unlocking of doors, opening and closing of frunk and trunk, honking the horn and flashing the headlights.

Battery SOC percentage is displayed at the top of the watchface. The battery icon changes to yellow, white, or green for charge levels less than or equal to 20%, greater than 20% and less than 80%, and 80% or greater respectively.

The area below the Battery SOC% shows the car's chosen nickname when the car isn't plugged in for charging  The app can manage multiple Lucid vehicles on the same Lucid account - tap the vehicle nickname text to select a different vehicle.

If the car is plugged in, an indicator replaces the nickname, and charging power is displayed. The charging power text and the lightning bolt to its left turn blue for RangeXChange discharging, Silver for zero charging power (plugged in but not charging), and green for charging. Tapping the charging current text issues a stop charging command to the car.

The Charging button at the bottom has a ring surround if the car is plugged in, color coded for charging speed. When pressed, the Charging button takes the user to other screens where charge level and charging speed can be set.

The Climate button at the bottom center shows climate status and when pressed, takes the user to Climate screens that control climate preconditioning and seat heating/cooling.

There's also a tiny yellow polling status dot that appears briefly at the very bottom of the screen each time the app polls Lucid's Cloud API. The app connects to the car through Lucid's cloud, so it works when both the car and the watch have access to the internet (WiFi or LTE) - the same mechanism as Lucid's phone app.

The Frunk, Door Locks, and Trunk buttons ask for user confirmation before sending their request to the cloud for safety.
